Questions

Straight answers.

What does Alta General Contracting do?
We are a general contractor in Edmonton, established in 1999. The work covers landscaping and concrete, commercial snow removal, and general contracting for residential, commercial, and industrial properties.
Where is the office?
8606 Yellowhead Trail NW, Edmonton, AB T5B 1G1. Call 780-965-9595 or email info@altagc.ca.
How do I request a quote?
Call 780-965-9595 or send the quote form. You can hold a site visit on that form. We call to confirm before the visit is final.
Is there a warranty?
Quality workmanship includes a 2 year warranty.
What are your office hours?
We do not publish office hours. For a site visit, choose a weekday or Saturday morning on the quote form, or call 780-965-9595. Sundays are closed for those visits.
Are you hiring?
Yes. Email a resume to info@altagc.ca.
